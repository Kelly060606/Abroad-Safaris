Essential Guide

10 Things to Know Before Your Safari

Preparation is key to an unforgettable African adventure. Here are our top expert tips.

01

Pack Light: Soft-sided bags are essential for small bush planes and safari vehicles.

02

Neutral Colors: Khaki, olive, and tan help you blend in and don't attract tsetse flies.

03

Tipping Culture: Small tips go a long way for guides and camp staff; ask us for a guide.

04

Early Starts: Wildlife is most active at dawn. Be ready for early morning wake-up calls.

05

Hydration: Drink plenty of water. Most lodges provide filtered or bottled water.

06

Patience: Nature doesn't have a schedule. Some of the best sightings come to those who wait.

07

Respect Boundaries: Always follow your guide's instructions regarding distance from wildlife.

08

Power Banks: Charging spots can be limited in remote camps; bring a portable power bank.

09

Local Phrases: Learning a few Swahili or Kinyarwanda words builds great rapport.

10

Unplug: While many lodges have Wi-Fi, take time to fully immerse in the sounds of the bush.
