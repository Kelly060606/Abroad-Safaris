Explore East Africa

Our Destinations

Discover the breathtaking landscapes, incredible wildlife, and rich cultures of Rwanda, Kenya, Tanzania, and Uganda.

🇷🇼

Rwanda

The Land of a Thousand Hills.

Volcanoes National Park

World-famous for thrilling mountain gorilla trekking experiences.

Akagera National Park

Rwanda's Big Five destination featuring stunning savanna and wetland lakes.

Nyungwe National Park

Ancient rainforest home to chimpanzees, primates, and an iconic canopy walk and zipline experience.

Lake Kivu

One of Africa's Great Lakes, perfect for relaxing and water sports.

Mount Bisoke HIKE

A thrilling climb to a beautiful crater lake at the summit of this dormant volcano.

King's Palace Museum

Traditional royal residence featuring the famous Inyambo cattle.

Ethnographic Museum

One of Africa's finest collections of pre-colonial cultural artifacts.

Community experiences

Immerse yourself in local culture through authentic village visits and activities.

🇰🇪

Kenya

The heart of the classic African safari.

Maasai Mara National Reserve

Legendary site of the Great Migration and dense big cat populations.

Amboseli National Park

Famous for large elephant herds with spectacular Mount Kilimanjaro views.

Lake Nakuru National Park

A bird-watcher's paradise famous for its massive flocks of pink flamingos.

Tsavo National Park

One of the oldest and largest parks, home to the famous "red elephants" and diverse wildlife.

Samburu National Reserve

A unique semi-arid wildlife reserve home to rare species like the Grevy's zebra.

Diani Beach

Pristine white sands and turquoise waters for a perfect coastline experience.

Mount Kenya

Africa's second-highest peak, offering incredible hiking and alpine scenery.

🇹🇿

Tanzania

Endless plains and majestic peaks.

Serengeti National Park

World-famous for its annual wildebeest migration and vast savanna plains.

Ngorongoro Crater

A stunning volcanic caldera harboring one of the highest wildlife densities.

Mount Kilimanjaro

The iconic snow-capped peak of Africa, offering world-class trekking.

Zanzibar Island

Exotic spice island with white-sand beaches and historic Stone Town.

Tarangire National Park

Famous for its ancient baobab trees and massive elephant populations.

Lake Manyara National Park

Diverse habitats featuring tree-climbing lions and flamingo-filled waters.

🇺🇬

Uganda

The Pearl of Africa.

Bwindi Impenetrable National Park

A mist-covered ancient forest harboring almost half the world's mountain gorillas.

Queen Elizabeth National Park

Diverse ecosystems featuring tree-climbing lions and the Kazinga Channel.

Murchison Falls National Park

Where the mighty Nile River forces its way through a narrow gorge with thunderous power.

Kibale National Park

The primate capital of the world, offering incredible chimpanzee tracking experiences.

Lake Bunyonyi

A serene, island-dotted lake perfect for canoeing and cultural discovery.

Rwenzori Mountains

The legendary "Mountains of the Moon", offering spectacular alpine scenery and trekking.

Mgahinga Gorilla National Park

Part of the Virunga volcanoes, home to gorillas and the rare golden monkeys.

Jinja

Adventure capital of East Africa and the historic Source of the mighty Nile River.
