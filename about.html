Our Mission

Authentic. Personal. Responsible.

Abroad Safaris was built on a simple idea. East Africa should be experienced in its most authentic form, while the landscapes and wildlife are respected and protected.

What started as a small team of passionate local guides has grown into a trusted safari company operating across Rwanda, Kenya, Tanzania, and Uganda. Even as we’ve grown, we’ve kept the same personal approach at the heart of everything we do.

We focus on authentic cultural immersion and meaningful community interaction, creating journeys that connect travelers with local traditions, stories, and everyday life in the places they visit.

We don’t just plan trips. We create experiences that stay with you. From trekking mountain gorillas in misty forests to watching the Great Migration unfold across the savanna, every journey is designed to feel personal, responsible, and truly unforgettable.

Distinct Moments

Our Unique Experiences

In addition to wildlife safaris, Abroad Safaris offers curated cultural immersion experiences that connect guests with living traditions, local artisans, and community life.

🌍 Cultural Immersion

Guests experience authentic Rwandan culture through direct interaction with local families, community groups, and artisans.

💃 Traditional Dance

Visitors learn and participate in Gakondo — traditional Rwandan dances led by local performers.

🎲 Traditional Games

Discover and play local games such as Igisoro and Gusimbuka Urukirampende alongside community members.

👘 Traditional Clothing

Guests have opportunities to wear traditional attire including Imikenyero and Kitenge while learning their cultural significance.

🧺 Local Arts & Crafts

Engage with artisans to discover Ikibo, Igiseke, and Imigongo art, supporting locally made crafts and fair-trade practices.

🤝 Community Interaction

These activities allow visitors to learn about Rwandan culture and interact meaningfully with local communities through shared experiences and respectful exchange.

Our Growth

The Journey So Far

From a local dream to an East African legacy.

2018

Founded in Kigali

Abroad Safaris is born in Rwanda with a focus on intimate, high-end gorilla trekking and conservation-led tourism.

2020

Expanding to Uganda

We flew our wings across the border, establishing operations in Bwindi and Queen Elizabeth National Park to offer seamless primate circuits.

2022

Reaching Kenya

Our fleet expanded into the great savannas of the Maasai Mara and Amboseli, bringing our signature personal touch to the classic safari.

2025

Tanzania & Beyond

Completing the East African circuit, we now operate full-scale expeditions in the Serengeti and Ngorongoro, serving travellers from around the world.
