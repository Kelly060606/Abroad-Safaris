Our Story

Rooted in East Africa

Discover the passion, expertise, and commitment to conservation that drives everything we do.

Our Mission

Authentic. Personal. Responsible.

Abroad Safaris LTD was built on a simple idea. East Africa should be experienced in its most authentic form, while the landscapes and wildlife are respected and protected.

What started as a small team of passionate local guides has grown into a trusted safari company operating across Rwanda, Kenya, Tanzania, and Uganda. Even as we’ve grown, we’ve kept the same personal approach at the heart of everything we do.

We don’t just plan trips. We create experiences that stay with you. From trekking mountain gorillas in misty forests to watching the Great Migration unfold across the savanna, every journey is designed to feel personal, responsible, and truly unforgettable.

Our Commitment

Travel With Purpose

We believe that tourism, when done right, is a powerful force for good.

Community First

Wildlife Conservation

Uncompromising Quality

Social Impact

Our Community Partners

We believe that tourism should directly benefit the people who call these landscapes home. Through our partnerships, we support sustainable livelihoods and cultural preservation.

Kamonyi Women Community

Empowering local women through sustainable crafts, education, and the preservation of heritage in the Kamonyi region.

Gorilla Guardians Village

A center for cultural preservation and former poacher rehabilitation near Volcanoes National Park.

Akagera Community Center

Focused on conservation education and creating harmony between the park and local residents.

Azizi Life

An ethical social enterprise empowering Rwandan artisans through fair-trade market access.

Cyamudongo Community

Active partners in the conservation of high-altitude forests and primate protection.

Fishing Community

Promoting sustainable aquatic livelihoods and environmental stewardship along Rwanda's lakes.

Our Growth

The Journey So Far

From a local dream to an East African legacy.

2018

Founded in Kigali

Abroad Safaris LTD is born in Rwanda with a focus on intimate, high-end gorilla trekking and conservation-led tourism.

2020

Expanding to Uganda

We flew our wings across the border, establishing operations in Bwindi and Queen Elizabeth National Park to offer seamless primate circuits.

2022

Reaching Kenya

Our fleet expanded into the great savannas of the Maasai Mara and Amboseli, bringing our signature personal touch to the classic safari.

2025

Tanzania & Beyond

Completing the East African circuit, we now operate full-scale expeditions in the Serengeti and Ngorongoro, serving travellers from around the world.
